# Second Monitor Games

The site at secondmonitorgames.com: short browser games with leaderboards and achievements, played on a second monitor during the workday.

## Language

**Guest**:
A player who has not signed in.
_Avoid_: anonymous user, visitor

**Daily checklist**:
The front page's view of which games a player has played today.
_Avoid_: dashboard, progress tracker

**Played marker**:
The word "Played" on the top left of a game card's picture.
_Avoid_: badge, status pill, tick
